How to read it

BUY supports adding long-horizon exposure. HOLD is neutral. SELL is unfavourable for fresh allocation.

The score compares each index against its own valuation history: 70% P/E percentile + 30% YoY implied EPS growth.

Signal thresholds assume implied EPS and the growth score are unchanged. They are model boundaries, not price targets.

Methodology⌄

Each index is evaluated against its own P/E history; the NIFTY 50 calibration is not imposed on other indices.

Current valuation percentiles use post-2021 month-end P/E observations. Historical backtests use quarter-end observations and separate pre/post-2021 P/E regimes.

Indices with insufficient P/E history or unavailable aggregate earnings are explicitly marked rather than assigned a synthetic signal.
