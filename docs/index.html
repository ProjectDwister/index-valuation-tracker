HOW TO READ IT

A slow-moving allocation signal

BUY means valuation and earnings conditions historically supported adding long-horizon NIFTY exposure. HOLD is neutral. SELL means the model is unfavourable for fresh allocation; it is not an instruction to short the index or liquidate a strategic portfolio.

The score is 70% post-2021 regime-relative valuation and 30% YoY implied EPS growth. The historical study uses quarter-end observations and no-lookahead expanding valuation percentiles for the signal backtest.
